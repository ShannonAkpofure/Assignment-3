School Subject Descriptions

Mathematics
Mathematics is the science of numbers, quantities, shapes, and their relationships using logical reasoning.
Science
Science is the systematic study of the physical and natural world through observation and experimentation
English language
In English grammar, a subject is the person, place, thing, or idea that does the action or is the focus of a sentence.
Art
The subject in art is the literal visual or idea that a piece depicts, representing the "what" of an artwork
Geography
Geography is the study of planet Earth, its physical features, and the people and societies that live on it.
